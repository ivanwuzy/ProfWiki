 Danny, Shizheng TAO 

Danny Tao is the CEO and founder of ePropulsion, a global leader in marine electric propulsion systems and services. He co-founded the company in 2012 with three college fellows while studying engineering and robotics. Tao’s passion for water sports and ocean protection led him to innovate the electric drive system for boats. Since the company launched its first electric outboard it has fast become a force to be reckoned with in the electric propulsion market with a presence across Europe, the US and Asia.

**Company Description**

ePropulsion has mastered technologies with proprietary intellectual properties that distinguish its products from competitors. Its products are efficient, reliable, eco-friendly, require almost no maintenance, and offer a unique boating experience. The brand is recognised across the world for its exceptional product quality, performance, technological innovations and extended distribution network.
