Professor Han, Kai
PhD(HK)
Assistant Professor
Hompage: https://www.kaihan.org
Research Group: https://visailab.github.io/
Professor Kai Han is an Assistant Professor in the
School of Computing and Data Science
at The University of Hong Kong, where he directs the
Visual AI Lab
. His research spans computer vision, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. His current research focuses on open-world learning, spatial intelligence, foundation models, generative AI, and their relevant fields. Previously, he was a Visiting Faculty Researcher at Google Research, an Assistant Professor at the University of Bristol, and a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Visual Geometry Group (VGG) at the University of Oxford. He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science at HKU, during which he also worked at the WILLOW team of Inria Paris and École Normale Supérieure (ENS). He is an IEEE Senior Member, is listed among the World's Top 2% Scientists by Stanford University, and serves as an Area Chair for major conferences including CVPR, ECCV, and ICLR.
