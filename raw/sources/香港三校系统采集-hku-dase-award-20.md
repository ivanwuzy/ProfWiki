Congratulations - HKSQ Student Project Competition 2026 - Implementation Excellence Award
Mr. Brian Tay Jian Shen received the Implementation Excellence Award
Congratulations to our student, Mr. Brian Tay Jian Shen from the Department of Data and Systems Engineering, who received the Implementation Excellence Award at the Hong Kong Society for Quality Student Project Competition 2026. His final-year project, titled “A Study of a Machine Learning-Based Forecasting Approach for Planning Building Energy Consumption and Carbon Credits in Hong Kong,” was supervised by Dr. H.H. Cheung.
