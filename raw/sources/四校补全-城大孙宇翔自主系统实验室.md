Introduction
About the lab:
The Autonomous Systems Lab (ASL) (自主系統實驗室) at CityU is directed by
Prof. Yuxiang Sun
(Chinese Name: 孫宇翔). Our lab studies AI-powered Robots and Autonomous systems, with a focus on Embodied AI and Autonomous Driving. Our mission is to create perception and control systems that enable autonomous robots to move, act and interact reliably in complex real-world environments at a large scale.
                  
                    Our lab has received funding support from a variety of sponsors, such as the University Grants Committee (
大學教育資助委員會
), Research Grants Council of Hong Kong (
香港研究資助局
), Hong Kong Innovation and Technology Commission (
香港創新科技署
), National Natural Science Foundation of China (
国家自然科学基金委员会
), Department of Science and Technology of Guangdong Province (
广东省科学技术厅
), Huawei (
华为

百度

HKPFS

Our keywords:
Robotics and Embodied AI, Robot Learning, Agentic Robotics, Vision-Language Action (VLA), Vision-Language Navigation (VLN), Mobile Manipulation, Autonomous Navigation, Autonomous Driving, Localization and Mapping, Robotic Perception and Control, Quadruped Robots, Humanoid Robots, Mobile Robots, etc.
Short biography of PI:
Yuxiang Sun is currently an Assistant Professor with the Department of Mechanical Engineering, City University of Hong Kong (CityU). He has been named among the
World’s Top 2% Most-Cited Scientists
by Stanford University for four consecutive years, from 2022 to 2025. He was selected as the
Global Top Chinese Young Scholars in AI
(Top 50 in AI+X),
AI华人青年学者榜
, by Baidu in 2022.
                    He serves as an Associate Editor of several prestigious journals and conferences: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems
(TITS)
, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering
(TASE)
, IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles
(TIV)
, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters
(RAL)
, IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation
(ICRA)
, and IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems
(IROS)
. He is a senior member of IEEE.
