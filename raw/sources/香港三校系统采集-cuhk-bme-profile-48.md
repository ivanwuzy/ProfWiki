About
ZHANG received his Bachelor degree in Department of Physics from Tsinghua University in 2018 and his PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2023, followed by a postdoctoral training at Singapore-MIT Alliance of Research and Technology and Tsinghua University before joining CUHK in 2025. His research interests focus on
scattering optical inspection
for biosensing, nanometrology, and manufacturing. He has published his work in high-profile journals, including Science, Nature Communications, Light: Science and Applications, Optics Letters, etc.
Professional Education and Training
2018 - 2023 Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, MIT

Research Interest
Development of the physical mechanisms and models for optical scattering phenomena.
Development of AI + Optics algorithm for scattering-related optical inspection;
Development of advanced optical inspection systems for biosensing, nanometrology, and manufacturing;
