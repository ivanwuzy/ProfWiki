I am an Assistant Professor at
Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Science (IIIS)
at
Tsinghua University
.
I am interested in the intersection between computer vision and robotics. Specifically, I want to explore how to utilize the prior knowledge we have from computer vision to do robot manipulation tasks both more efficiently and effectively. This not only involves understanding how to use the previous visual experiences but also potentially needs re-designing robotic learning algorithms to better handles the visual states.
Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. degree from
UC Berkeley
, advised by Prof.
Trevor Darrell
and B.E. from the
Computer Science Department
at
Tsinghua University
.
I welcome highly motivated and strong undergrad students and Ph.D. candidates to join our lab. If you are interested, please feel free to send me your resume and transcript via email.
