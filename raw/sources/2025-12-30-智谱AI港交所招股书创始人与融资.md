## History, Development and Corporate Structure

Our history can be traced back to June 2019, when our Company was founded by a team with deep academic roots at Tsinghua University, including Dr. Liu, Dr. Li, Dr. Tang, Dr. Xu, Dr. Zhang and Mr. Wang. Our Company draws on the academic rigor and innovative spirit of its founding team to advance cutting-edge developments in AI.

We completed eight rounds of Pre-IPO Investments and had raised funds of over RMB8,360 million.
