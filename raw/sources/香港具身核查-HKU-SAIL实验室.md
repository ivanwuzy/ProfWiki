Our general research interests cover the broad area of Computer Vision, Embodied AI, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence, with special emphasis on building super intelligent systems. Our research goal is to utilize artificial intelligence techniques to make machines perceive, understand, imagine, and interact with the surrounding environment, and ultimately make high positive impacts on various fields. Our current research interests and focus include: Embodied AI, Multimodal AI, Spatial AI, and Generative AI.
