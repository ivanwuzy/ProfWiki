Hi, I'm Yinghao!
I am an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the
HKUST
, leading the
HKUST XU Lab
.
Previously, I was a staff research scientist at
RobbyAnt
, leading a research group working on world models and embodied AI.
Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University working with
Prof.
Gordon Wetzstein
.
My current research focuses on 3D computer vision, generative AI and embodied AI. I am
particularly interested in developing powerful generative models to perceive and generate compact
representations of environments and leverage them to understand, interact with, and reason about the  physical world.
