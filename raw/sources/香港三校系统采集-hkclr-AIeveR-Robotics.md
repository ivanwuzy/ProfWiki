AIeveR Robotics
AIeveR Robotics Limited is dedicated to developing world-leading embodied intelligence robotic products. The company brings together experts from fields such as artificial intelligence, robotics, and machine vision, forming a core team that spans R&D, operations, and sales.
It develops advanced embodied AI foundation models and high-precision 3D sensor technologies, integrating them into dual-arm manipulators and legged robotic platforms to create 3D perception and embodied intelligence robotic systems. Its target markets include 3C manufacturing, automotive components, logistics, retail, and agriculture. Currently, the company’s products are undergoing pilot or scaled deployment across these sectors, providing clients with innovative intelligent robotics solutions.
Innovations and Technologies
High-speed, high-accuracy and robust 3D imaging sensors
Safe and low-cost force-controlled robot arms
Energy-efficient legged robots
Air3D: An Al model for robust and real-time 3D perception
AirMan: An Al model for general robot manipulation
