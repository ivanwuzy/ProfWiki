CoRe Lab
The
Co
gnitive
Re
asoning (
CoRe
) Lab is affiliated with the
School of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences
and the
Institute for Artificial Intelligence
at PKU. Our projects span three directions:
Abstract Reasoning:
analogy, causality, IQ, number sense, physics, and tool-use.
Visually Grounded Reasoning:
affordance, container, functionality, hoi, intent, scene parsing, scene reconstruction, and scene synthesis.
Interactive Reasoning:
communication, manipulation, TAMP, teaming, UAV, VR, and XAI.
