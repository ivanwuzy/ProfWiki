Ruiqian Nai Ruiqian Nai
                  佴瑞乾 I am a fourth-year PhD student at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences, Tsinghua University , advised by Prof. Yang Gao . Previously, I earned my B.S. degree from the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University . I am currently an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon FAR (Frontier AI & Robotics) , working on humanoid whole-body manipulation, advised by Guanya Shi , C. Karen Liu , Carmelo Sferrazza , and Rocky Duan . Research Interests My research focuses on robot learning , which I approach as a deeply systematic challenge spanning hardware understanding , data acquisition , and scalable training . Currently, I focus on humanoid whole-body manipulation , spanning both robotic foundation models and humanoid control . Pursuing general intelligence on today's humanoids is like building autonomous driving on a unicycle — my goal is to make that as tractable as driving a car, by tackling both ends of the stack together. / Scholar / CV / Twitter / Github Selected Publications (* indicates equal contribution) Your browser does not support the video tag.
