Chen, Ho
Prof. Chen, Ho
PhD UC Berkeley
Professor
Chair of Cybersecurity
Homepage:
https://sec.hku.hk
Professor Chen is a Chair Professor in the Musketeers Foundation Institute of Data Science and Department of Computer Science. He directs the JC STEM Lab of Intelligent Cybersecurity. He is a fellow of IEEE.
Research Interests
Computer security, machine learning, program analysis and testing.
Selected Publications
Lyu, Y., Xie, Y., Chen, P. & Chen, H. Prompt Fuzzing for Fuzz Driver Generation. ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (CCS), 2024.
Li, Q., Guo, Y., Zuo, W. & Chen, H. Towards Evaluating Transfer-based Attacks Systematically, Practically, and Fairly. Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2023.
Chen, P., Xie, Y., Lyu, Y., Wang, Y. & Chen, H. HOPPER: Interpretative Fuzzing for Libraries. ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (CCS), 2023.
Chen, P. & Chen, H. Angora: Efficient Fuzzing by Principled Search. IEEE Symposium on Security & Privacy (S&P), 2018.
Meng, D. & Chen, H. MagNet: a Two-Pronged Defense Against Adversarial Examples. ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (CCS), 2017.
Division of Computer Science,
School of Computing and Data Science
The University of Hong Kong
Pokfulam Road, Hong Kong
