Biography
Dr. Fu Zhang received his B.E.
degree in Automation from the University of Science and
Technology of China (USTC), Hefei, Anhui, China, in 2011,
and the Ph.D. degree in Controls from the University of
California, Berkeley, CA, USA, in 2015. His Ph.D. work
focused on self-calibration and control of micro
rate-integrating gyro sensors. In 2016, Dr. Zhang shifted
his research to the design and control of Unmanned Aerial
Vehicles (UAVs) as a Research Assistant Professor in the
Robotics Institute of the Hong Kong University of Science
and Technology (HKUST). He joined the Department of
Mechanical Engineering, the University of Hong Kong (HKU),
as an Assistant Professor from Aug 2018. He was promoted to
an Associate Professor with tenure in 2024. His current
research interests center on aerial robotics and intelligent
autonomous systems, with a focus
on spatial intelligence, robotic control, embodied
AI, and aerial platform and manipulation.
