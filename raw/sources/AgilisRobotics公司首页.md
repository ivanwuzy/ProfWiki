The Next Generation of Minimally
Invasive Surgery
Agilis Robotics is committed to transforming endoluminal surgery with our highly miniaturized and flexible robotic instruments.
Our mission is to empower surgeons to easily and intuitively remove cancers in both the urinary tract and the gastrointestinal tract – all without any external incisions.
About
Breaking the size barrier for truly flexible surgical robotic instruments
Our instruments are ultra-thin and fully flexible with a diameter between 2.8 mm to 3.5 mm and lengths of up to 1.8 m, allowing surgeons to access and remove tumours in areas ranging from the bladder to deep within the colon.
