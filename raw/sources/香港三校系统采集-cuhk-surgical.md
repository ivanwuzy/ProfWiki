Surgical Robotics and Instrumentation Laboratory
Mission
We are a medical technology-focused lab at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) that develops
enabling technologies and systems for
image-guided robotic surgery
millimeter-scale
continuum/soft robots
other medical robotics applications such as surgical simulation, rehabilitation, etc.
We push the technological boundaries through research in
mechanism design and system
integration
medical imaging/perception
modeling and control (fundamental
algorithms and practical frameworks)
sensing and actuation
(new materials/techniques, fabrication, model, etc.)
human-robot interaction
The lab promotes collaboration and communication between multidisciplinary engineering students and clinicians, and considers it a critical component towards developing any medical/surgical robotic system that woul
d improve surgical outcomes
. We intend to advance fundamental and practical robotics research, and always work towards translating the technology from laboratory to clinical settings.
The lab is also fully committed to a diverse, fun, inclusive and interdisciplinary environment that allows individual member to voice his/her unique perspectives, feel respected and valued, and pursue his/her academic/career goals.
