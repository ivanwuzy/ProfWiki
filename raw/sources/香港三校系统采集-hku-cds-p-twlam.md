Lam, T.W.
Professor Lam, Tak-Wah
BSc
CUHK
; MS, PhD
Washington
Professor
Deputy Director, School of Computing & Data Science
Executive Director, School of Computing & Data Science (Shanghai)
Professor TW Lam joined HKU in 1988 upon receiving his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Washington. Currently, he serves as the Deputy Director of the School of Computing and Data Science (CDS) in Hong Kong and concurrently as the Executive Director of CDS in Shanghai. In this latter role, he orchestrated the groundbreaking establishment of CDS Shanghai, which was HKU’s historic first initiative to deliver its undergraduate (alongside postgraduate) education outside Hong Kong through an innovative one-school-two-cities model. Prior to this, he served as the Head of the Computer Science Department from 2018 to 2023, during which he spearheaded a massive expansion of the department and propelled it to a historic high in the UGC Research Assessment Exercise (
RAE
). His leadership portfolio also includes establishing the HKU-SCF FinTech Academy as its founding Executive Director from 2020 to 2023, alongside serving two terms as Associate Dean of the Faculty of Engineering across the 2000s and 2020s.
Professor Lam's research ranges from theoretical analysis of algorithms to big data analytics for health informatics. He has published over 250 research articles in international conferences and journals, and the most impactful ones come from the work in bioinformatics software; notably, his seminal paper on the MEGAHIT assembly tool has surpassed 10,000 citations, and he was named a Charivate Highly Cited Researcher in 2017. Driven by a focus on tackling industrial challenges with intricate algorithmic techniques, his work has secured a number of ITF grants, and in 2014, he co-founded a startup company to advance clinical bioinformatics software technologies.  Professor Lam is passionate about teaching, especially in inspiring students to learn theoretical subjects. He has received several teaching awards from the Department and the Faculty, as well as a University-level research student supervisor award.
Research Interests
Algorithms, Bioinformatics & Big Data Analytics
