About Me
The
InfoBodied AI Lab
at HKU is building the next generation of embodied AI systems — robots and autonomous agents that can perceive complex 3D environments in real time, discover actionable concepts from unlabelled data, and learn generalizable manipulation policies that operate reliably in the open world.
I am an Assistant Professor at the
School of Computing and Data Science (CDS)
, HKU, jointly appointed by the Department of
Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)
and the HKU Musketeers Foundation
Institute of Data Science (IDS)
. I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at
Stanford University
with
Leonidas J. Guibas
and received my Ph.D. from the
University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)
with
Stefano Soatto
. Earlier, I obtained my Master's and Bachelor's degrees from
KAUST
and
USTC
, respectively.
Our research tackles core challenges in
robot manipulation
,
3D scene understanding and real-time reconstruction
,
visuomotor control
, and the
grounding of large vision-language foundation models
in the physical world — with a particular focus on reducing dependence on costly human annotation. A unifying thread across this work is the use of efficient mutual information estimators as a principled tool for representation learning, concept discovery, and policy optimization, forming the basis of our research program in
InfoBodied AI
. Our work has appeared at CVPR, NeurIPS, ICLR, ECCV, ICML, CoRL, ICRA, and SIGGRAPH, among others.
Ph.D. students, postdocs and interns!
We are looking for highly motivated researchers who want to work on open problems at the frontier of embodied intelligence. Backgrounds in computer vision, robotics, machine learning, or related areas are all welcome. Please reach out via
email
with a CV and a brief description of your research interests.
