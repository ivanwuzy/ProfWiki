## About Me

I am now a fourth-year Ph.D. student at Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences, Tsinghua University, advised by Professor Jingzhao Zhang. I am also an intern of Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute. Previously, I did my undergraduate studies at School of Data Science, Fudan University, advised by Professor Luo Luo.

## Selected Awards

- National Scholarship (for PhD students), 2024-2025, Tsinghua University.
- National Scholarship (for undergraduates), 2019-2020, Fudan University.

## Research

I have broad interests in modern optimization theory, with a particular focus on game-structure optimization. In my representative work ⭐, I have collaborated with my excellent coauthors to solve many fundamental problems in this area, such as the oracle complexity of convex, nonconvex, minimax, and bilevel optimization.
