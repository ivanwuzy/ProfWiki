Mission
At Archon Robotics, we're building a Humanoid Foundation Model (HFM) for whole-body intelligence — the foundational AI layer that lets humanoid robots move, perceive, reason, and act in the real world.
We aim to become a leading embodied AI company and build the "OpenAI for Humanoid Robotics" in China.

What We're Building
Overview
We're developing a Humanoid Foundation Model (HFM) that enables robots to perform whole-body tasks requiring simultaneous mobility, manipulation, perception, and decision-making.
Core capabilities include:
01
Whole-body locomotion and manipulation
02
Long-horizon task planning and execution
03
Multi-modal perception and reasoning
04
Real-world robot learning from large-scale data
05
Generalizable robot skills across environments and embodiments
Our platform combines foundation model training, robot data infrastructure, simulation systems, and deployment on physical humanoid robots.
Where We Are
Demo / Early Product Validation
We've already developed internal prototypes and demonstrations of whole-body robot intelligence, and we're actively scaling robot data collection, model training, and real-world deployment.
Over the next 12 months, we plan to expand our robot fleet, train larger foundation models, and establish pilot deployments with strategic robotics and enterprise partners.
