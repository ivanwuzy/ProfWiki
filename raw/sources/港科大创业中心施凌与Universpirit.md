Fellow
Prof. Ling SHI
Professor of Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering, HKUST
Program Director of MSc in Electronic Engineering, HKUST
Associate Director of HKUST-DJI Joint Innovation Laboratory
Co-founder of Universpirit Innovation Limited
Prof. Ling Shi received his B.E. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) in 2002 and  Ph.D. degree in Control and Dynamical Systems from The California Institute of Technology (Caltech) in 2008. He is currently a Professor in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at HKUST. His research interests include cyber-physical systems security, networked control systems, sensor scheduling, event-based state estimation, and multi-agent robotic systems (UAVs and UGVs). He served as the General Chair of the 23rd International Symposium on Mathematical Theory of Networks and Systems (MTNS 2018). He is a member of the Young Scientists Class 2020 of the World Economic Forum (WEF), a member of The Hong Kong Young Academy of Sciences (YASHK), and he is an IEEE Fellow. He co-founded Universpirit in 2022 to promote STEAM education.
Company Description
The mission of Universpirit Innovation Limited is to become a global leader in STEAM education.Through the development of a distinctive interactive curriculum based on an innovative ultra-light weight fixed-wing flight platform, we are dedicated to nurturing the next generation of young talents and future leaders.
