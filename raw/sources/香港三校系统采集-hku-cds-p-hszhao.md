Professor Zhao, Hengshuang

CUHK
Assistant Professor
Homepage:
https://www.ai.hku.hk/~hszhao

https://sail.ai.hku.hk
Prof. Hengshuang Zhao is an Assistant Professor at the School of Computing and Data Science of The University of Hong Kong and an NSFC Excellent Young Scientist. Before that, he was a postdoctoral researcher at Massachusetts Institute of Technology and University of Oxford. His general research interests cover the broad area of Computer Vision, Embodied AI, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence, with special emphasis on building super intelligent systems. He has published more than 100 papers in top venues like CVPR, NeurIPS, and TPAMI. His research works have been cited about 60,000+ times, one of his first-authored papers gets more than 20,000 citations, and six of his first-authored papers get more than 1,000 citations. He has won several champions in competitive international challenges, and many awards and hornors like the WAIC rising star award, bright star award, youth outstanding paper award, Ant InTech award, CVPR best demo honorable mention award, 100 young pioneers in artificial intelligence, 35 Innovators Under 35 China by MIT Technology Review, and is recognized as one of the most influential scholars in computer vision by AI 2000 and is ranked as world’s top 2% scientists by Stanford University. He serves as an Area Chair for CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, and an Associate Editor for IEEE TPAMI.
