## 原文

Founded in July 2025, KinetixAI is a full-stack embodied AI company at the forefront of foundation model development for robotics. We are a team of engineers and researchers driven by a shared belief: that robots can only meaningfully enter the human world by adopting human-like bodies, interaction paradigms, and learning trajectories.

This conviction defines our architecture. KinetixAI is committed to the highly anthropomorphic humanoid form factor, building end-to-end capabilities across four domains — hardware embodiment, cognitive intelligence, dexterous manipulation, and data infrastructure. KaiBot is our highly anthropomorphic humanoid robot platform. KAI Hand enables precision dexterous manipulation. KAI Halo Lite and the KAI EGO Dataset establish the data pipeline for grounded, real-world learning. SMASH stress-tests perception and decision-making under high-speed dynamic conditions. KAI Embodied AI Infra provides the foundational infrastructure that drives our continuous iteration at scale.
