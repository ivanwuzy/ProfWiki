Professor Weijia Wen is Professor Emeritus in the Department of Physics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. He earned his BS (1982) and MS (1988) degrees at Chongqing University. He completed his PhD (1995) degree in Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Science, Beijing. He was a postdoctor in HKUST and UCLA during the period of 1995-1999. He joined HKUST since 1999. Professor Wen's main research interests include soft condensed matter physics, electrorheological (ER) and magnetorheological (MR) fluids, field-induced pattern and structure transitions, micro- and nano-fluidic controlling, advanced functional materials including microsphere and nanoparticle fabrications, thin film physics, band gap materials, metamaterials and nonlinear optical materials.
