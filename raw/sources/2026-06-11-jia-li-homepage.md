## Home

Jia Li (李佳)

I am the tenure-track Assistant Professor of College of AI at Tsinghua University. I obtained my PhD degree from Peking University in 2025, supervised by Prof. Zhi Jin and Prof. Ge Li.

I lead the Tsinghua AI Agent Group and work closely with Prof. Shi-Min Hu. Recently, our group focuses on the following questions:

- How to improve the foundational capabilities of AI agents? Such as reasoning, memory, tool-calling, efficiency, safety, and multi-agent collaboration.
- How to build powerful AI agents for real-world tasks? Such as software engineering agents, scientific research agents, etc.

The page states that the group is looking for highly-motivated research interns, master students, PhD students and Postdoctoral researchers.
