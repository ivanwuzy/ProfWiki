Xinge Yu Research Group – Lab of Soft Bio-Electronics

Our research lab makes efforts on developing flexible electronics and bio-electronics, and conducts multidisciplinary research addressing challenges in practical applications, such as biomedical electronics with compatible physical and chemical properties, and real-time health monitoring. To develop novel bio-integrated electronics, we are focusing on three parts, developing and engineering functional materials; designing and fabricating difference scale devices for sensing, controlling and actuating; integrating and optimizing system level electronics.
