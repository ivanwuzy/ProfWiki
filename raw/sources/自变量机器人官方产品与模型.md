X Square Robot develops general-purpose embodied intelligence robots with high-precision manipulation capabilities. We are one of China's first companies to pioneer end-to-end solutions for general-purpose embodied intelligence.
Our WALL-A model, part of the Great Wall embodied foundation model series, integrates autonomous perception, decision-making, and high-precision manipulation, demonstrating world-leading performance across multiple scenarios.
X Square Robot adheres to a philosophy of tightly integrating hardware-software iterative development, including independent design and constant refinement of our robotic platforms to optimize for multimodal large-model control. This ensures the highest precision manipulation and stable performance in open real-world environments.
Our robots are currently deployed in scenarios requiring complex, multi-step task execution.
