I am an Assistant Professor of
Electronic and Computer Engineering
and
Computer Science and Engineering
at
The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
. My research lies in the interdisciplinary areas of artificial intelligence and medical image analysis, aiming at advancing healthcare with machine intelligence. Specifically, I work on making machine learning more reliable, human-compatible, and statistically rigorous, and am especially interested in applications in healthcare.

Before joining HKUST, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at
Stanford University
, working with Prof.
Lei Xing
. I obtained my Ph.D. degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof.
Pheng-Ann Heng
and Prof.
Chi-Wing Fu
.
I am looking for self-motivated Postdoc/PhD/RA/Interns. Feel free to drop me an email with your CV.
If you are an HKUST student interested in doing research with me, please send me an email.
