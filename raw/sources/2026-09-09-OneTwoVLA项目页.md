Long-Horizon Task Planning OneTwoVLA excels at handling long-horizon manipulation tasks. It consistently demonstrates the ability to understand the physical scene, generate correct plans, track task progress accurately, and produce precise actions. This allows OneTwoVLA to successfully complete challenging tasks such as hotpot cooking, tomato-egg scramble, and cocktail mixing. The task instructions for each synthetic image are categorized into two types: visual grounding tasks and long-horizon planning tasks.
