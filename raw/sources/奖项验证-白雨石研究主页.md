Selected Publications
My research interests primarily lie in large language models (LLMs), covering long-context LLM, LLM evaluation and safety, and multimodality. Recently, I have been focusing on designing more efficient and comprehensive LLM architectures that push beyond traditional architectures, particularly in long-context and agentic scenarios.
