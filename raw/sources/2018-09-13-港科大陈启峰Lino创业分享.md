Build YouTube on the Blockchain: Entrepreneurship Experience Sharing 
by Co-founder of Lino Network
Date: 19 Sep 2018 (Wednesday)
Time: 6-7pm
Venue:
LTA
Title: Build YouTube on the Blockchain: Entrepreneurship Experience Sharing by Co-founder of Lino Network
Speaker: Dr. CHEN Qifeng, Assistant Professor, CSE HKUST and Co-founder, Lino Network
Abstract:
Lino was co-founded by Stanford and Berkeley alumni in July 2017. We 
raised $20 million from top investors to build YouTube on the blockchain. 
We have 30 full-time employees in Silicon Valley. Currently, our 
decentralized live streaming platform has more than half a million monthly 
active users, and the Lino blockchain is released and under testing.
As a co-founder of Lino, I will share my experience in founding and 
running a startup. How did we find co-founders for a startup? How did we 
identify an area with emerging technologies for running a startup? How did 
we raise funding from venture capital at the right time? How did we 
recruit employees and develop products?
Biography:
Dr. Qifeng Chen is an assistant professor of CSE and ECE at HKUST. He 
received a PhD in computer science from Stanford University in 2017, and a 
bachelor's degree in computer science and maths from HKUST in 2012. His 
research interests are computer vision, machine learning, optimization, 
and computer graphics. Four of his papers were selected for full oral 
presentations in ICCV'15, CVPR'16, ICCV'17, and CVPR'18. He co-founded 
Lino to build "YouTube on the blockchain." In 2011, he won the 2nd place 
worldwide at the ACM-ICPC World Finals. He earned a gold medal at IOI 
2007.
