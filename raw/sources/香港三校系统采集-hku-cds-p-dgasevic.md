Gašević, Dragan
Professor Gašević, Dragan
PhD
Chair Professor
Office location:
Dragan Gašević is Chair Professor in the Faculty of Education and the School of Computing and Data Science at the University of Hong Kong. His research interests center around data analytic, AI, and design methods that can advance understanding of self-regulated and collaborative learning.
Previously, he was Distinguished Professor of Learning Analytics in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence of the Faculty of Information Technology and the Founding Director of the Centre for Learning Analytics (CoLAM) at Monash University. He was also a Professor and the Sir Tim O’Shea Chair in Learning Analytics and Informatics in the School of Informatics and the Moray House School of Education and Co-Director of the Centre for Research in Digital Education at the University of Edinburgh. He was the Canada Research Chair in Semantic and Learning Technologies and Professor in the School of Computing and Information Systems at Athabasca University.
Professor Gašević is a founder and served as the President of the Society for Learning Analytics Research (SoLAR), the world’s leading research and professional organization in learning analytics. He served as a founding program chair of the International Conference on Learning Analytics & Knowledge (LAK), general chair in 2016, founding program co-chair of the Learning Analytics Summer Institute (LASI), and founding editor of the Journal of Learning Analytics and
Computers & Education: Artificial Intelligence
.
From 2019 to 2025, Professor Gašević was recognized as the national field leader in educational technology in
The Australian
. He led the EU-funded SHEILA project, which received the Best Research Project of the Year Award from the Association for Learning Technology. In 2022, he received the Lifetime Member Award, the highest distinction of the Society for Learning Analytics Research (SoLAR), and was named a Distinguished Member of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).
His full list of publications can be found
here
.
Research Interests
Learning analytics, artificial intelligence in education, human-AI interaction, technology-enhanced learning, self-regulated learning, collaborative learning
