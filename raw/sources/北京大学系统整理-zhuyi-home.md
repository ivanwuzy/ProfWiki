Biography
Dr. Yixin Zhu received a Ph.D. degree (‘18) from UCLA advised by Prof.
Song-Chun Zhu
.
His research builds interactive AI by integrating high-level common sense (
functionality
,
affordance
,
physics
,
causality
,
intent
) with raw sensory inputs (pixels and haptic signals) to enable richer representation and cognitive reasoning on objects, scenes, shapes, numbers, and agents.
Dr. Zhu directs the PKU
CoRe Lab
, working on abstract reasoning, visually grounded reasoning, and interactive reasoning.
