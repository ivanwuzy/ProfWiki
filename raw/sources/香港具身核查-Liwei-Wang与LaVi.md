Liwei Wang
Liwei Wang
I am an Assistant Professor in Computer Science and Engineering department at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). Before coming to HK, I have worked for more than two years as a Senior Researcher in Tencent America at Bellevue, US.
I got my PhD from Computer Science Department, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by Prof.
Svetlana Lazebnik
. The Language and Vision (LaVi) Lab, which I founded at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at CUHK, conducts research in World Models, Language+Vision, and multi-modal models. I serve as an Editorial Member of IJCV and Area Chairs in multiple Computer Vision and NLP conferences.
