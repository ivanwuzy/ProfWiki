Cartesius Robotics
Cartesius Robotics Limited is dedicated to the R&D and commercialization of intelligent mobile robots for operation in natural environments. The company's core technologies include wide-angle fisheye stereo 3D cameras, visual SLAM, autonomous path planning, and multi-sensor fusion perception, showcasing its full-stack R&D capabilities from mechatronics design to algorithm and software development.
Its main business includes the R&D and sales of smart lawn-mowing robots (for gardens, parks, embankments), robot controllers, and visual perception modules, targeting high-end landscaping and public service markets in Europe, the U.S., Japan and Australia. The company has delivered multiple batches of mowing robots for use in complex environments such as reservoirs and is engaged in deep collaboration with several industry leaders.
Innovations and Technologies
A robust and low-cost visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (vSLAM) technology for navigating in lawn environments and avoiding obstacles like trees, flowers, small animals, etc
A low-cost and real-time smart stereo vision for reliable recognition of objects, animals, and scenes, and highly precise depth perception in the environment
Target Applications
House lawn maintenance
Clearing weeds in large farmlands
Urban landscaping projects such as regular lawn trimming
