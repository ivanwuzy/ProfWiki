Professor of Tsinghua University
Biography
Xing Junliang is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Tsinghua University, specializing in visual situation awareness and game adversarial decision-making. He has authored over 70 CCF-A papers, accumulating over 17,000 citations. Notably, he has been honored with three significant international conference paper awards and emerged victorious in six technical challenges both nationally and internationally. Mr. Xing secured the first prize in the Wu Wenjun Artificial Intelligence Natural Science Award, standing out as the inaugural awardee. His professional journey encompasses roles as a professor at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, chief professor of four courses including Game Theory, researcher at the Institute of Automation of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (with promotion) and doctoral supervisor.
