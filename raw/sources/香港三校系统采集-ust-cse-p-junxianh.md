Biography
Junxian He is an Assistant Professor at the Department of Computer
Science and Engineering (CSE), the Hong Kong University of Science and
Technology (HKUST). He received his Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University
and his bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His main
research interests are in natural language processing. His work has been
recognized by the best demo paper nomination in ACL 2019, the most
influential paper list in ICLR 2022 (paper digest), and Baidu PhD
fellowship.
