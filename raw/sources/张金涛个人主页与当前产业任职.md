## About Me

I am a five-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Jun Zhu. Feel free to call me Qifan Zhang (张棋番), my preferred nickname.

In the past, I was a visiting scholar at Sky Computing Lab, UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Joseph E. Gonzalez, and also worked closely with Ion Stoica.

Currently, I am leading the Streaming Video Generation and Inference Infrastructure at Shengshu Technology (生数科技).
