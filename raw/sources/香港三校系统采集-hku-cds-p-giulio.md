Chiribella, G.
Professor Chiribella, Giulio
PhD
Pavia, Italy
Professor
Associate Director (Research & Graduate Studies) of CDS
Homepage:
https://www.cs.hku.hk/~giulio
Research group homepage:
http://qift.weebly.com/
Croucher Senior Research Fellowship 2018-19
CIFAR-Azrieli Global Scholar.
Member of the Editorial Board of Journal of Physics A.
Member of the Standing Committee of the International Colloquia on Group Theoretical Methods in Physics (ICGTMP).
Fellow of the National Virgilian Academy of Sciences, Letters and Arts, Italy.
Research Interests
Quantum information theory, quantum networks, information-theoretic foundations of quantum mechanics.
Selected Publications
G. Chiribella, Y. Yang, and J. Huang, Universal Superreplication of Unitary Gates , Phys. Rev. Lett. 114, 120504 (2015)
G. Chiribella and C. M. Scandolo, Entanglement and thermodynamics in general probabilistic theories, New J. Phys. 17, 103027 (2015)

G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, P. Perinotti, and B. Valiron, Quantum computations without definite causal order, Phys. Rev. A 88, 022318 (2013)
G. Chiribella and J. Xie, Optimal design and quantum benchmarks for coherent state amplifiers, Phys. Rev. Lett. 110, 213602 (2013)
G. Chiribella, Perfect Discrimination of No-Signalling Channels via Quantum Superposition of Causal Structures , Phys. Rev. A 86, 040301, Rapid Communications (2012)
G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, and P. Perinotti, Informational Derivation of Quantum Theory, Phys. Rev. A 84, 012311 (2011)
G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, and P. Perinotti, Probabilistic Theories with Purification, Phys. Rev. A 81, 062348 (2010)
A. Bisio, G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, S. Facchini, and P. Perinotti, Optimal Quantum Learning of an Unknown Unitary Transformation , Phys. Rev. A 81, 032324 (2010)
G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, and P. Perinotti, Theoretical Framework for Quantum Networks, Phys. Rev. A 80 022339 (2009)
G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, and P. Perinotti, Transforming Quantum Operations: Quantum Supermaps, EPL 83, 30004 (2008)
G. Chiribella, G. M. D'Ariano, and P. Perinotti , Quantum Circuits Architecture, Phys. Rev. Lett. 101, 060401 (2008)
